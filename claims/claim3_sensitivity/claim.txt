Claim 3 - Hyperparameter Sensitivity

1. Claim description
   20-round CIFAR-10 sensitivity sweep of GMSA hyperparameters: alpha_mom,
   kappa, and MAD multiplier, plus the default center point.

   during the review phase and described in the Author Response. The artifact
