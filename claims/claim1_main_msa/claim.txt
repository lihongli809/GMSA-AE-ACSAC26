   rate 0.20 on CIFAR-10 and FeMnist. The artifact bundles the saved per-round
   canonical results (Round, Acc, Loss, Rej, TPR, FPR) and, for GMSA, the LSA
   score matrix NPY.

4. Verification procedure
   The provided verifier script (run.py) checks the bundled canonical results
   by:
   - confirming the required CSV and NPY files are present;
   - validating the CSV header against the expected columns;
   - validating the recorded round counts (100 for CIFAR-10 and 50 for
     FeMnist).

   This is canonical-result integrity verification, not full experiment
   reproduction.
